abstract: "Generating realistic and functional indoor scenes requires a nuanced understanding of spatial configurations and human-centered design principles. We propose InteriorAgent, an LLM-agent-driven framework for text-to-3D indoor scene generation that encodes interior design principles through a novel scene description language and uses synthesis and optimization tools to ensure aesthetics, functionality, and ergonomics. In a user study, participants strongly favor InteriorAgent's scenes over prior state-of-the-art methods, and the framework further enables applications such as language-based scene editing and seamless integration of new tools."
